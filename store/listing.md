## Full description (4000 chars max)

ProTrack Golf is the driving-range companion for new golfers. Record each ball as you hit it, build a personal yardage chart for every club in your bag, and watch your distances improve session by session.

**One tap per ball at the range**
Pick your range, the clubs you're hitting today and your bucket size, then it's one tap per shot. A big balls-left counter, a distance slider you can use with a glove on, and a floating add button mean you spend your time hitting, not typing.

**A recap after every session**
Finish a bucket and see how each club went: shots, average, best, and whether you hit it further than last time. Words that keep showing up in your notes ("slice", "thin", "tempo") are surfaced so you can spot what to work on.

**My Bag: your own yardage chart**
Add the clubs you carry and how far you hit them. Every range session updates the average, best and latest distance for each club, with a trend chart per club so you can see progress over weeks, not just days.

**Built for the range, not the office**
A pure white, high-contrast design you can read in bright sunlight. Large tap targets. No accounts, no sign-in, no ads.

**Your data stays on your phone**
Sessions, clubs and locations are stored on the device only. Nothing is uploaded and nothing is tracked. Android backup keeps your data when you change phones.

**Also**
- Add a past session by hand — date, range, bucket, shots per club, notes and photos
- Keep the ranges you practise at, and see how much you've hit at each
- Undo a mis-tapped shot, edit distances, delete anything with a confirmation

Whether you've just bought your first set or you're finally getting serious about practice, ProTrack Golf turns a bucket of balls into numbers you can improve on.
