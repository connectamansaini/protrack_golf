# ProTrack Golf — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 9 September 2026

ProTrack Golf ("the app") is a driving-range companion for golfers. This
policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The
short version: everything you enter stays on your phone, and we never see
it.

## What the app stores

The app stores only what you type or choose inside it:

- **Practice sessions** — date, the range you practised at, bucket size,
  the clubs you hit, the distance of each shot, and any notes you write.
- **My Bag** — the clubs you carry and the distance you enter for each.
- **Locations** — the names of the ranges you practise at.
- **Photos and videos** — if you attach media to a session, the app copies
  the file you picked into its own private storage.

All of this is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is
not uploaded to us or to anyone else.

## What the app does not do

- It has **no user accounts** and no sign-in.
- It makes **no network connections**. There is no server, no analytics,
  no crash reporting and no advertising SDK.
- It requests **no Android or iOS permissions**. Choosing a photo or video
  uses the system picker, which shares only the single file you select.
- It does not read your location, contacts, or anything outside its own
  storage.

## Device backup

On Android, the app allows the standard Android backup service to include
its data in your device backup, so your sessions and clubs survive a move
to a new phone. That backup is managed by your Google account under
Google's own privacy terms; ProTrack Golf does not have access to it. You
can turn device backup off in your phone's Settings.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any session, club or location inside the app. Uninstalling
the app removes all of its data from the device (a device backup, if you
have one, is cleared by the system according to your backup settings).

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on
our side.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal
information from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting or transmitting data, this policy will
be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above
will change.
